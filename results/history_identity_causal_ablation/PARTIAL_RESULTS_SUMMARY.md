# History-identity causal ablation — stopped early

This is a **partial, seed-1 result**, not a completed four-arm comparison. The user set a new screening criterion on 2026-09-23: the candidate should improve over current-code FedPhoenix by at least 1–2 percentage points within the first 500 rounds. The H arm missed that criterion, so the S/P processes and launcher were stopped. B0 and H completed 1000 rounds; S stopped at 466 and P at 448. No new tuning or experiments were run after this decision.

All arms used the frozen VGG/CIFAR-10, Dirichlet alpha 0.3, seed-1 E5 configuration. B0 is current-code FedPhoenix. H/S/P used the same frozen B0 history score bank and common-support policy; H is therefore a **controlled off-policy** same-client-history arm, not the original on-policy E5 run. The score bank remains local and is not included in the Git commit; it is regenerated by the B0 producer.

## Incomplete controls

Across the **same observed rounds 151–448**, mean accuracy was B0 72.0758, H 71.9932, S 72.0121, and P 72.2821. These are interim, non-prespecified comparison windows. S/P observed peaks and final recorded accuracies are not 1000-round peaks or final outcomes. No H>S, H>P, or necessity-of-identity conclusion is warranted.

## Correctness and data provenance

- The repository's 83 unit tests passed. The preflight manifest records exact rho=0 10-round parity and the deterministic active smoke test.
- B0 and H had matching global-model SHA-256 hashes for rounds 1–150. S and P also matched the B0 prefix through round 150.
- Selected clients, task seeds, and total reset-slot counts matched B0 for every round observed in each arm. Through the 448-round common interval, H/S/P had identical targeted slot counts, targeted client counts, fallback counts, and common-support hashes.
- The frozen matching schedule had 4,032 eligible client events in rounds 151–770; 4,028 had an exact-age peer and 4 used unified fallback. Donor and target were always different.
- B0 and H completed with zero failed client updates. No traceback or CUDA OOM was found in the experiment stderr logs before the user-directed stop.
- `B0/` and `H_same/` contain complete configs, round metrics, summaries, and compressed task traces. `S_shuffled/` and `P_population/` contain partial configs, round metrics, and compressed task traces. The manifest records `stopped_by_user` and the exact stopped process snapshot. Raw logs, local score bank, smoke/parity raw outputs, and uncompressed task traces are intentionally not committed.

The original E5-vs-controlled-H reset-index-delta analysis and full H/S/P causal comparison were not finalized after the user-directed stop. They must not be inferred from this partial report.
